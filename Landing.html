Egg Fried Rice

Ingredients :-

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably cold rice)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 tomato (optional)
  • 1 green chili
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt
  • Oil or butter
  • Spring onion/coriander for garnish

Steps :-

  1. Heat oil in a pan
  2. Add onion and chili. Cook until golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and tomato. Cook till soft.
  4. Add turmeric, chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
  5. Push everything to one side and crack the eggs in. Scramble them.
  6. Add rice and mix well on high heat for 2-3 minutes.
  7. Garnish with coriander or spring onion.
